The AI Pipeline

From single photo to 3D geometry in seconds.

Measure Mate combines deep convolutional pose estimation with parametric human body models (SMPL) to calculate tailor-accurate physical circumferences.

STEP 01

Image Ingestion

You upload a front photo in fitted clothing. The pipeline centers, crops to 224×224, and normalizes lighting and pixel scales.

Auto-cropping & Scale Normalization
STEP 02

Landmark Detection

Convolutional networks identify key anatomical joints: neck, shoulders, elbows, wrists, hips, knees, and ankles.

2D Skeletal Keypoint Mapping
STEP 03

3D Mesh Recovery

The HMR neural model predicts shape (β) and pose (θ) parameters to fit a 6,890-vertex SMPL 3D surface mesh to your body.

Parametric 3D SMPL Fitting
STEP 04

Contour Slicing

Using your true height, the model scales 3D vertices to physical centimeters and slices cross-sections for chest, waist, and hips.

Geodesic Slicing & Metric Calibration
